## Flights: Toronto to Brazil

### Direct Flight Options

**Air Canada (Recommended for Pets)**
- Route: Toronto (YYZ) → Sao Paulo Guarulhos (GRU)
- Flight Time: ~10-11 hours direct
- Frequency: Daily flights
- February 2026 Prices: ~CAD $800-1,000 per adult round trip
- Infant (lap): ~10% of adult fare (~$80-100)

**Booking Tips:**
- Book 2+ months in advance for best rates
- February is LOW SEASON = cheapest flights
- Check: [Air Canada](https://www.aircanada.com), [KAYAK](https://www.ca.kayak.com), [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.com)

### Pet Cargo with Air Canada

**Important:** Pets cannot travel in-cabin to Brazil. Must use cargo.

**Air Canada AC Animals Program:**
- Contact: Air Canada Cargo (AC Animals)
- Booking: Must book through IPATA-registered agent
- Cost: Varies by dog size/kennel (~$500-1,000+ per dog)
- Kennel: Soft carriers NOT allowed for cargo - must use IATA-approved hard kennels

**Holiday Embargo:** December 15 - January 12 = NO pet cargo. Plan accordingly.

**Restricted Breeds (Cannot Fly):**
- Pit Bulls (all types)
- Mastiffs (including Brazilian Fila)
- Rottweilers
- Wolf Dog Hybrids

## Pet Travel Requirements

### Before Leaving Canada (CFIA/USDA Requirements)

**Timeline: Start 6+ weeks before departure**

1. **Microchip** (if not already done)
   - Must be ISO 15-digit chip readable by universal scanner
   - Must be implanted BEFORE rabies vaccination

2. **Rabies Vaccination**
   - Must be current (within 1-3 years depending on vaccine)
   - Dogs must be 3+ months old
   - Wait 21 days after vaccination before travel

3. **Additional Vaccinations Required:**
   - Distemper
   - Hepatitis
   - Leptospirosis
   - Parainfluenza
   - Parvovirus (DHLPP)
   - Bordetella
   - *Administer 2+ weeks before departure*

4. **Parasite Treatment**
   - Internal and external parasites
   - Must be done within 15 days of travel

5. **Health Certificate**
   - Issued by CFIA-accredited veterinarian
   - Must be within 10 days of flight
   - Get CFIA endorsement

**Documents to Carry:**
- Original rabies certificate
- CFIA-endorsed health certificate
- Vaccination records
- Microchip registration

### Entering Brazil

**Good News:** Brazil has relatively simple requirements
- No import permit required
- No quarantine
- No microchip requirement (but have one anyway for return)
- Just need endorsed health certificate

### CRITICAL: Returning to Canada

**Brazil is classified as HIGH-RISK for rabies by CFIA**

**Requirements for Re-entry:**
1. Valid rabies vaccination certificate (issued by licensed vet)
2. Vaccination must be current (within 3 years)
3. Dogs must be 3+ months old
4. Present all documents to CBSA officer on arrival

**Recommendation:**
- Keep ALL original documents safe throughout trip
- Get rabies booster in Brazil if vaccination expires during stay
- Check CFIA AIRS database before return for any updated requirements

## Important Information

### Visa Requirements

**Already obtained per user**

For reference: Brazilian e-Visa costs $80.90 USD, valid for 10 years, allows 90-day stays.

### Healthcare

- **Public (SUS):** Free emergency services but long waits
- **Private:** Faster, higher quality - RECOMMENDED
- **Travel Insurance:** ESSENTIAL - get comprehensive coverage including:
  - Medical evacuation
  - Trip cancellation
  - Baggage loss
  - Pet coverage (if available)

### Money

- **Currency:** Brazilian Real (BRL/R$)
- **Exchange Rate:** ~1 CAD = 4.0-4.5 BRL (check current)
- **ATMs:** Widely available (Banco24Horas network)
- **Credit Cards:** Widely accepted, Visa/Mastercard preferred
- **Tip:** Use credit card for better exchange rate, avoid airport exchanges

### Internet/Communication

- **Mobile:** Buy local SIM (Claro, Vivo, TIM) for data
- **WiFi:** Confirm accommodation has fiber/high-speed for remote work
- **Speed Needed:** Minimum 25 Mbps for video calls, 50+ Mbps recommended
- **Backup:** Portable WiFi hotspot or phone tethering

### Safety Tips

- Florianopolis is one of Brazil's safest cities
- Avoid displaying expensive equipment in public
- Use hotel safes for valuables
- Keep copies of documents separate from originals
- Register with Canadian embassy
- Download offline maps (Google Maps, Maps.me)
